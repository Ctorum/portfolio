# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers (Brazil and international), freelance or contract clients, and engineering peers from the open-source and Linux community. They arrive from a CV, a LinkedIn link, or GitHub, skim for 30 seconds to judge seniority and taste, and want a fast path to contact or to real work.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Vitor Hugo Cabral Rezende, a Brazilian full-stack and mobile engineer (Node/TypeScript, Python/FastAPI, React Native, AWS/GCP serverless, AI). It exists to turn visitors into conversations: interviews, project inquiries, or peer contact. Success is a visitor who remembers it as the considered portfolio of a careful engineer. Bilingual (en, pt).

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, considered. Confidence through restraint: the work and the writing carry it, not effects. Voice is plain, specific, first-person, no buzzword stacks.

## Anti-references

- The current look: dark zinc background with blue radial glow.
- Identical card grids for projects and skills.
- Walls of tech badge/chip pills.
- Pulsing glows, typewriter headline, floaty decorative motion.
- Generic AI/SaaS template tropes (gradient text, glass cards, hero metrics).

## Design Principles

1. Editorial over dashboard: typography and whitespace do the work a template would do with cards and glows.
2. Specific beats impressive: concrete roles, stacks, and outcomes in plain sentences instead of tag clouds.
3. Hierarchy by rhythm: vary scale, density, and layout per section so the page reads like a set document, not repeated modules.
4. Motion is rare and earns its place: one or two deliberate moments, fully respectful of reduced motion.
5. Easy to contact: the path to email/GitHub/LinkedIn is always one glance away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes, theme toggle that follows system preference, full keyboard navigation with visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, correct `lang` per locale (en, pt).
